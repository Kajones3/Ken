This explains what Parkfare (pricingthemagic.com) collects, why, and what
you can do about it. Short version: **we collect as little as we can, we
don't sell it, and there are no ad trackers on the Site.**

## What we collect

**If you just search (no account):**
- **The route and month you searched** (for example "Atlanta to Orlando,
  March 2027"). This is stored with **no name, no email, no account and no
  IP address** attached. We use it to decide which airfares to look up
  overnight, so popular routes get better prices.
- **Your preferences in your own browser** (the last options you picked),
  kept in your browser's local storage. They never leave your device.

**If you create an account:**
- Your **email address** and a **scrambled (hashed) version of your
  password** — we can't read your actual password.
- A **sign-in cookie** that keeps you signed in. It's the only cookie we set,
  and it's needed for the Site to work.
- Anything you choose to save: **home airport, saved searches (including
  numbers you typed in), extra trip costs, attraction picks, and any
  pass/points details** you enter.
- Whether you have **Plus** and until when. [When payments are live: your
  payment is handled by Stripe; we receive a confirmation, not your card
  number.]

**For security:**
- When someone tries to sign in, we briefly keep the **email address tried
  and the IP address** it came from, so we can block password guessing after
  too many wrong attempts. These records expire after [15 minutes / a short
  time].

**If you use "use my location" or the city search (driving mode):**
- Your IP address (for "use my location") or the city you typed is sent to a
  free lookup service — [ip-api.com] and [OpenStreetMap Nominatim] — to find
  your approximate starting point. We keep the typed place names and their
  map coordinates to avoid looking them up twice; we don't link them to you.

## How we use it

- To run the Site: show prices, keep you signed in, save and reopen your
  searches, make your PDF.
- To email you about your account (confirm your address, reset your
  password) and, if you're a Plus member with a confirmed address, about
  new Disney deals.
- To keep the Site secure.

We **don't** sell your information, share it with advertisers, or use it to
build an advertising profile.

## Your choices

- **See or delete your data:** email [CONTACT EMAIL] and we'll send you a
  copy or delete your account and everything saved with it within [30] days.
- **Stop deal emails:** use the "Stop these emails" link at the bottom of

If you live in California, the EU/UK or another place with privacy laws,
you have the rights those laws give you — including access, correction and
deletion — and you can use them the same way, by emailing us.

## Children

Parkfare isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect
their information. (Children's **ages** that parents enter to price tickets
aren't linked to any child's identity.) If you think a child has created an
account, email us and we'll delete it.
