    What it would take to make these numbers real

    Flights. Travelpayouts / Aviasales gives fare search plus affiliate booking links and is open to solo developers at no cost. Duffel is the alternative if you ever want to sell the ticket yourself — $3 per confirmed order, no IATA accreditation needed. Amadeus is no longer an option: its self-service developer portal was decommissioned on 17 July 2026.

    Hotels. Travelpayouts covers both on-property and off-property inventory through Booking.com and Agoda. Expedia Rapid has better Disney-resort coverage but requires a corporate entity and a trading history.

    Tickets. No Disney park has a public pricing API, at any of the six resorts. The practical route is a maintained price table per resort, refreshed against each resort's published date-based calendar, plus affiliate feeds from authorized resellers. Disney has confirmed airline-style dynamic ticket pricing is coming to Walt Disney World and Disneyland after its trial at Disneyland Paris — which makes a stored price table go stale faster, and makes the "when should we go" question this app answers considerably more valuable.

    Currency. Local prices are converted at illustrative rates. A live FX feed replaces them.

    Want to make it cheaper?
